Privacy

Last updated: 2026-05-28

The Nūr al-Īḍāḥ Reader is a static web app. There is no server-side account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking pixels. We do not have a backend; the entire app is shipped as static files from GitHub Pages.

What we store on your device

The app uses your browser’s localStorage to remember:

  • Display preferences (Arabic on/off, word-by-word translation, full translation, ḥarakāt, dark mode) under the key nai.settings.v1.
  • Your word bank — the Arabic words you save and their translations — under the key nai.wordbank.v2.
  • If you sign in with Google, a small profile snapshot (name, email, picture) under nai.google.profile.v1, used solely to display the signed-in state in the toolbar. Access tokens are never written to disk; they only live in memory and disappear when you close the tab.

You can clear this data at any time via your browser’s “Clear site data” controls or by clicking Clear all in the Word bank drawer.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to sign in with Google, your word bank is written to a hidden, app-only folder inside your own Google Drive (using the drive.appdata scope). This folder is not visible in the normal Drive UI and is accessible only to this application.

  • We do not request access to any other files in your Drive.
  • We do not have a backend, so your data never passes through any server we operate. It travels directly between your browser and Google.
  • Revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions immediately stops the app from being able to read or write the synced file.

Network requests

The page itself fetches extracted.json (the corpus) from the same origin as the site. Web fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. If you opt in to Google sync, requests are made to accounts.google.com (sign-in) and www.googleapis.com (Drive). No other outbound requests are made by the app.
